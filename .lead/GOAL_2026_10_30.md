## Consolidation rules
- Before starting ANY step: search for prior work on it. Cite what you found in the brief or commit, or say "none found" and say where you looked.

- Cleanup as you go:
  - Remove worktrees that are proven landed or harvested AND clean, with `git worktree remove`; report each one.
  - Ask before removing a dirty worktree or one with unharvested commits.
  - No recursive deletes in a command. Clear only your own worktree's `.avra-cache`, and never touch another worktree's `build/`.
