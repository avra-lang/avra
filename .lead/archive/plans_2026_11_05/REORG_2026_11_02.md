## Loop rules

1. LOCAL FIRST. Shared compiler at ../avra-shared/avra-<sha> (s-gate builds it per hd-clean tip). `make try` in your worktree, `./avra-try test <file>`, `./avra-try check <pkg>`. Each step <=2 min.
2. GitHub only to gate an integration sha: at most one run per lane at a time, a background wait that wakes you, `gh run view` at most every 10 min, never a loop.
3. Commit small, at least hourly. Push, send the sha to s-gate. Rebase on origin/hd-clean after every integration.
4. Decide designs yourself under ONE_PATH and CLAUDE.md. Report decisions in your commit message. Ask the lead only for owner decisions.
5. Never end a turn without a background wait or the next step started.
6. Never: --no-verify, rm in command text, pkill by pattern, deleting worktrees.
