  - It answers `choose`, `generate`, `link` and `retrieve`. It is the ONE resident model, and every lane shares it.
  - The client refuses a service running different code, so restart it after editing `cascade.py`. Warn the users of the socket first.

  - Eval harness: `gym/evalrun.py`.
